OUR STORY

Teaching is human.
AI is the co-pilot.

ClassSense AI was born from a simple frustration: teachers couldn't see their students' faces clearly enough through tiny Zoom thumbnails to know if anyone was lost. We built the system we wished existed.

// OUR MISSION

No student should feel invisible in an online class.

Online education amplified access — but it also amplified isolation. Students who would have raised their hand in person now suffer silently behind muted mics and off cameras. ClassSense AI gives teachers the superpower to see through the screen.

// THE TECHNOLOGY

How the AI reads the room

Multimodal Signal Analysis

ClassSense combines video posture cues, audio participation patterns, chat activity, and response latency into a unified engagement score — updated every 4 seconds.

Adaptive Emotion Classification

A lightweight emotion model classifies each student across 5 states: Focused, Excited, Confused, Thinking, and Bored — calibrated to online learning contexts, not just general emotion datasets.

Contextual Suggestion Engine

Suggestions are generated in natural language based on the combination of class-wide mood, individual signals, and what's happened in the last 5 minutes of the session.

Privacy-First Design

All analysis runs locally in the browser. No student video is stored, uploaded, or shared. Everything stays in the session and disappears when the tab is closed.

// OUR JOURNEY

How we got here

2023 · The Problem

A teacher noticed nobody raised their hand

During a post-pandemic online class, an educator realised she had no idea which of her 12 students were lost. They all had cameras off.

2024 · The Prototype

First version built in a weekend hackathon

A rough prototype that tracked mic activity and facial expressions. Teachers loved it immediately. The insight feed was born.

2025 · Beta Launch

200+ teachers joined the beta program

Multi-student tracking, emotion classification, and the quick-action toolbar were all shaped by real teacher feedback over 8 months.

2026 · Today

ClassSense AI — open and free for every teacher

A fully browser-based, zero-install, privacy-first tool. No account needed. Just open and teach.
